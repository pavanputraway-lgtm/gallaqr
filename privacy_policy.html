1. Who we are

GallaQR is a mobile application that lets a merchant create UPI QR codes for a fixed amount. GallaQR is only a QR code generator. It does not process payments, hold money, act as a payment system, or confirm transactions. All payments happen inside the customer's own UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, etc.) through the standard UPI network operated by NPCI and the banks.

2. What data we collect

GallaQR is built to collect as little as possible. The following information is stored only on your own device and is not sent to us or to any server:

  • Your merchant/shop name and UPI ID (VPA), which you enter yourself.
  • The QR codes you generate (amount, optional note, date/time) for your own history and reports.
  • Your app settings (theme, QR expiry time, language).
  • Your subscription/trial status.

To send you helpful notifications and to understand app usage, GallaQR uses Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging and Analytics). The following limited, non-identifying data is collected:

  • A device notification token, so we can send you offers, reminders and updates. No login or account is required.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage events — for example which screens are opened and which features are used, along with general app performance and diagnostics.

This data does not include your name, phone number, UPI ID, or any QR / transaction records — those remain only on your device. Firebase data is processed by Google in accordance with its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can stop this collection at any time by turning off notifications for the app in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, bank login, card details, or payment credentials. We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.

3. Permissions we use

  • Camera: only when you choose to scan an existing UPI QR to fill your UPI ID during setup.
  • Storage / Photos: only to save a QR image to your gallery when you tap "Download".
  • Notifications: to send you offers, reminders and important updates. You can turn this off anytime in your device settings.
  • Internet: used to load fonts for PDF reports, validate Google Play subscriptions, and enable Firebase notifications and anonymous analytics. Your on-device data (shop name, UPI ID, QR records) is never transmitted.
  • Biometric: only to unlock the app if you turn on App Lock.

4. Legal basis & your consent (DPDP Act, 2023)

By using GallaQR you consent to the limited on-device processing and the anonymous Firebase analytics / notifications described above, which are necessary to provide and improve the service you requested. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off notifications, deleting your data in the app (Settings → Clear history), or uninstalling the app.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, and to grievance redressal. Because your personal data lives on your device, you can edit it in Settings or erase it by clearing history / uninstalling. Firebase notification and analytics data is removed when you uninstall the app. For any request, contact us at pavanputra.way@gmail.com.

6. Data retention

Your personal data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not keep server copies of your shop data. Anonymous Firebase usage data is retained by Google for a limited period per Google's standard retention settings and is not linked to your identity.

7. Children

GallaQR is intended for merchants and is not directed at children under 18.

8. Third-party services

GallaQR uses the device's UPI apps and the system share sheet, may load fonts from Google Fonts for PDF generation, uses Google Play Billing for subscriptions, and uses Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging and Analytics) for notifications and anonymous usage analytics. These are governed by their own privacy policies, including Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Payments & subscriptions

GallaQR is free to download. Optional Premium features are sold as subscriptions billed securely by Google Play. We never collect or store your payment details — these are handled entirely by Google Play.
